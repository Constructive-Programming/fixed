# Constructive

[Constructive](https://www.constructive.dev/) is the consultancy behind
Fixed. It works with platform teams on type-driven architecture: fewer
representable states, fewer bugs, and a type-level lens on design reviews.

The practice covers architecture reviews, embedded senior engineers in Scala,
Rust and TypeScript, agent-native development workflows, and workshops. Fixed
makes the same argument in a language: move the choice of representation out
of the program and into the compiler, and a whole class of programs stops
compiling.
